## Problem: Projectile Motion

A ball is launched from ground level with an initial speed of 20 m/s at an angle of 30° above the horizontal. Ignore air resistance and assume that gravity is constant.

Use $g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$ to calculate:

1. The horizontal and vertical components of the initial velocity.
2. The total flight time.
3. The maximum height reached by the ball.
4. The horizontal range, which is the distance traveled before the ball hits the ground.
